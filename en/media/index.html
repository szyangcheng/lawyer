2026-04-14YicaiEmployee rights & business risks

Haidilao’s service model under pressure: three days’ wages to appease a customer

Yang Cheng · Interview summary

Requiring employees to buy gifts at their own expense to settle complaints may amount to disguised wage deductions. Customer complaints do not necessarily cause a business loss. Employers should not force staff to fund gifts and shift ordinary business risks onto them.

Source: Yicai · Reporter: Feng Xiaoxin

2026-03-10Sina Finance · 5D InvestigationPrepaid purchases & business closures

Restaurant closures: who should refund customers’ prepaid balances?

Yang Cheng · Interview summary

A sudden closure preventing refunds may constitute a breach of contract. Failure to notify customers or arrange services and refunds may indicate consumer fraud. In bankruptcy, prepaid balances generally constitute ordinary unsecured claims, without priority in a restructuring.

Source: Sina Finance · 5D Investigation · Reporter: Xu Yuanlei

Xinba cancels a livestream: what happens to the promised RMB 100 million compensation?

2024-03-14YicaiFood advertising & consumer rights

Weight-loss foods borrow the profile of popular medicines; company denies a connection

Yang Cheng · Interview summary

Promoting weight-loss foods through drug or pharmaceutical company names may breach rules on medicines, advertising and unfair competition. Product information should be truthful and complete, without suggesting that a food has a medicine’s therapeutic effects. Affected consumers may seek compensation according to law.

2023-08-08YicaiHealthcare compliance & lecture fees

Medical anti-corruption: examining doctors’ lecture fees

Yang Cheng · Interview summary

Lecture fees reflecting professional qualifications, services provided and genuine academic exchange, and unrelated to sales promotion, may be remuneration for services, subject to employer approval, tax and other requirements. Payments disguised as lecture fees remain commercial bribery if the applicable legal elements are met.

Source: Yicai · Reporters: Lin Zhiyin, Qian Tongxin

2023-07-30YicaiSupervisory investigations & persons involved

Why was Sailong Biotech’s chairman detained for a supervisory investigation?

Yang Cheng · Interview summary

The public information available at the time did not establish whether the person investigated was subject to supervision. When investigating public officials’ work-related offences, supervisory authorities may also investigate non-public officials suspected of bribery, introducing bribes or jointly committing such offences.
